Common questions

Before you call.

Can you quote from photos?

Often, yes — for insurance claims we regularly assess from dated site photographs and confirm everything with a site measure before work commences. For an urgent make-safe figure, photos are usually enough to get things moving.

Do you deal with the insurer directly?

Yes. We're set up to submit quotes to the owner's insurer or the at-fault driver's assessor, answer scope questions, and handle variation approvals in writing before proceeding.

Is your quote the final price?

Our quotes are fixed-price and itemised, with any contingency stated separately. The price only changes if the scope changes — and variations are always confirmed in writing first.

The insurer wants a second quote. What now?

That's routine. We're happy to provide the itemised scope for comparative quoting, and to walk the assessor through any differences between quotes — most of the time it comes down to scope, not price.
